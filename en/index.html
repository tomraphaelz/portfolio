Beyond
Figma

I'm 22 and based in Osnabrück, where I study Media & Interaction Design at Osnabrück University of Applied Sciences. Outside of projects and user research, I play basketball with friends, enjoy long walks, closely follow what's happening in the AI space, and am a passionate gamer.

Basketball taught me early on that teamwork beats individual performance — and I bring that same mindset to the design process. Long walks are one of my best sources of creative thinking: distance from the screen, ideas settle. AI interests me not as hype, but as a serious tool — I test what actually helps and drop what's only meant to impress. Gaming has sharpened my UX instincts more than almost anything else: no medium is as precise about feedback, onboarding, and motivation as a well-made game.

Good design comes from a genuine understanding of people. For me, thinking beyond interactions in UX means taking life away from the screen just as seriously as life on it.
